How the system works
  • The soil sensor node (ESP32 and capacitive sensor) reads moisture at root depth and sends it over Wi-Fi.
  • The pump controller (ESP32 and relay) compares it with the start and stop levels, 35% and 60%.
  • Below 35% the relay switches on the submersible pump. It draws water from the reservoir and pushes it through the pipe to the drip lines.
  • At 60% the pump stops and a message goes to the phone.
  • From the phone you can switch to manual mode and start or stop the pump.
